# SportsHub System Evaluation Questionnaire

**Based on the ISO/IEC 25010 Software Quality Model**

**Surigao del Norte National High School (SNNHS)**

---

## Instructions to Respondents

Thank you for taking part in the evaluation of **SportsHub**, the Progressive Web Application for athlete records and sports activity management. Before answering, please make sure you have interacted with the major modules of the system during the testing session.

For each statement below, indicate your level of agreement by checking the box that best reflects your response. There are no right or wrong answers — your honest assessment will help the researchers determine the quality of the system and identify areas for improvement.

**Please answer all items.**
